How does IntiShield work?

Three simple steps. Your original file never leaves your device.

  1. Your media stays private

    Choose a file on your device. It is processed locally and is not uploaded.

  2. A fingerprint is created

    Your browser turns it into a digital fingerprint. Only the fingerprint is shared.

  3. Participating platforms can detect matches

    Platforms compare uploads with protected fingerprints and can block or review matches.

What is IntiShield?

A tool for people who are depicted in intimate media and want to prevent its unauthorized publication or redistribution.

Unlike a normal file hash, a perceptual fingerprint can help identify visually similar copies, such as resized or compressed versions.

We protect your privacy

The fingerprint is generated on your device and is the only thing shared with IntiShield.

The service is designed to collect the minimum data needed and not to keep a gallery of your original media.

What it cannot do

Detection depends on platform integration. IntiShield cannot control every website.

It does not delete content that is already online.

Who is IntiShield for?

If all of the following apply, you can use IntiShield.

  • I am depicted in the media.
  • The media contains nude, semi-nude, or sexual content.
  • I was 18 or older when the media was created.
  • I do not consent to the media being published or redistributed online.
