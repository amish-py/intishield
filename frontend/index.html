How does IntiShield work?

Three simple steps. Your original file never leaves your device.

  1. Your media stays private

    Choose a file on your device. It is processed locally and is not uploaded.

  2. A fingerprint is created

    Your browser turns it into a digital fingerprint. Only the fingerprint is shared.

  3. Participating platforms detect matches

    Platforms compare uploads with protected fingerprints and block or review matches.

What is IntiShield?

A tool for people who are depicted in intimate media and want to prevent unauthorized publication.

Unlike simple file hashes, perceptual fingerprints identify visual copies like resized or compressed media.

We protect your privacy

The fingerprint is generated on your device and is the only item shared with IntiShield.

We do not store your original photos or videos.

What it cannot do

Detection depends on platform integration. It does not automatically delete content already published online.

Who is IntiShield for?

If all of the following apply, you can use IntiShield:

  • I am depicted in the media.
  • The media contains nude, semi-nude, or sexual content.
  • I was 18 or older when the media was created.
  • I do not consent to the media being published or redistributed online.
